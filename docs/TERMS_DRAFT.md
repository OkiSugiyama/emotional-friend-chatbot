## Intended use

The provisional public MVP is for adults 18 and over. Emotional Friend offers
general conversational support. It is not a therapist, medical device,
diagnostic service, emergency service, or substitute for qualified care.

If someone may be in immediate danger, they should contact local emergency
services or a trusted nearby person. The service does not promise rescue and no
human responder is monitoring conversations.

## Accounts and demo mode

Registered users are responsible for access to their account. Demo mode stores
conversation data only in the current browser and clears it after 30 minutes of
inactivity. Demo data does not transfer automatically when an account is
created.

## Acceptable use

People must not use the service to violate law, harm others, probe another
person's private data, bypass service limits, introduce malicious content, or
abuse the AI/provider infrastructure. Automated or excessive access may be
limited to protect availability, safety, privacy, and cost.

## AI and expression-estimation limits

AI replies and facial-expression estimates can be incomplete, inaccurate, or
inappropriate. An expression estimate is an uncertain local signal, not proof
of an internal emotion. People remain responsible for decisions they make and
should seek appropriate qualified or emergency help when needed.

## Availability and changes

The service relies on third-party authentication, storage, hosting, and AI
providers and may be interrupted. Material model, prompt, privacy, or safety
changes require the review and rollout controls defined in the release process.
